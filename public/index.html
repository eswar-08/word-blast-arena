💣 How to Play Word Blast Arena

1
Watch the Syllable: When it's your turn, a 2 or 3-letter prompt appears (e.g. PRO).
2
Type & Pass: Type a valid word that contains that syllable (e.g. PROJECT, APPROVE) and hit Enter before the bomb explodes!
3
Survive: If the timer hits 0s, BOOM! You lose 1 heart. Last player with hearts remaining wins the Crown 👑!
4
React & Taunt: Spectators and players can tap emojis anytime to stream floating reactions onto everyone's screens!
